# Invariants of P4

The rows of the [invariant register](../invariants.md) that roadmap part P4 added ([readiness](../readiness/P4.md)). Same columns and rules as the register; ids `INV-P4-<n>`.

| ID | Guarantee | State | Where | Pinned by | Closed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-P4-1 | Each revision the lead writes on the quick path without a blocking finding of its own is read once against the researcher's own words by one call with no tools, whose input holds only their words (the conversation, their answers to the lead's questions, the values they set on the charter) and that revision of the charter; on the formulator's tier model, else the lead's, within the campaign's model budget; its instructions, input, output (checked against its schema) and usage are saved as a `charter_reading` record before the lead or the note is given it | code | `balsamic/notes/reading.py` `assemble()`, `read()`; `balsamic/notes/service.py` `write_charter()` | `tests/balsamic/test_quick_reading.py::test_the_reading_holds_the_researchers_words_and_the_charter_and_nothing_else`, `tests/balsamic/test_quick_reading.py::test_a_revision_with_a_blocking_finding_of_the_leads_is_not_read` | |
| INV-P4-2 | `problem_note.confirm` on the quick path is refused until the lead's last write has a completed reading and each difference it found is settled by the researcher: the charter is right (kept with who and when, in `problem_note.settle` or in the confirmation's `right`), or the lead is asked to fix it, which starts a turn of the lead naming it and leaves the confirmation to the revision the lead writes next | code | `balsamic/notes/reading.py` `check()`, `settle()`; `balsamic/notes/service.py` `confirm()` | `tests/balsamic/test_quick_reading.py::test_a_difference_refuses_the_confirmation_until_the_researcher_settles_it_and_who_settled_is_kept`, `tests/balsamic/test_quick_reading.py::test_the_confirmation_settles_what_the_researcher_marks_right_in_the_same_action`, `tests/balsamic/test_quick_reading.py::test_asking_the_lead_starts_a_turn_naming_the_difference_and_its_next_write_is_read_again`, `tests/balsamic/test_quick_reading.py::test_the_confirmation_waits_for_a_reading_that_runs_and_asks_for_one_that_never_ran` | |
