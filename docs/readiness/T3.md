# T3 — Review specialists as one-shot calls; decide the harness plan's D2

State: implemented and tested (PR to be opened, 2026-10-28); the three-arm live comparison, and with it the final default and
D2, are Hoseung's.

## What was wrong

Layer: evidence. The two review specialists that only read, `methodology_specialist` and `skeptical_domain_analyst`, were
Pi sessions with read tools: they could read the reviewers' verdicts before giving their own reading, and what they saw was
spread over their turns. The harness plan's D2 (merge the specialists into one analyst) was open.
Invariant: INV-12 (where updated); INV-T3-1, INV-T3-2, INV-T3-3 (added).

## Checked

- `tests/test_one_shot_specialists.py` (5 tests): a methodology specialist's input holds the methods, the representation,
  the proposal, the resources and a cited trial's status, result and metrics (without its series), and no verdict on its own
  proposal or another, nor a cited proposal's reviews; its instructions keep "A good result", name no tool and hold its
  schema. Through a scheduling pass a skeptical analyst's result is saved on the session and the run, the call holds exactly
  the prompt sent, the asker's notice points at `/output`, a message to it is refused, and one asked about the campaign at
  large gets no proposal. A reply outside the schema saves nothing. The switch returns a specialist, and the reviewer, to a
  session and refuses an unknown name. With `analyst`, one merged call answers for the skeptical analyst.
- Tests that assumed a session specialist now use a literature specialist (`tests/test_agent_discussions.py`'s steering
  message, `tests/test_one_shot_reviews.py`'s "other assistants keep their sessions"). `tests/test_roles.py` passes.
- `scripts/run_lead_evals.py --fake`: all 19 scenarios as before (S15 is its known failure). S12 passes in all three arms
  (`--one-shot-roles proposal_reviewer`, the default, `--one-shot-roles proposal_reviewer,analyst`), with the Review roles
  table showing session, one_shot and analyst.
- The full Python suite in parallel and the launcher's tests as `claude`: counts in the PR.

## Not done

- **The comparison.** It needs a model, so it is Hoseung's (acceptance test below). Until it runs, both specialists are one
  call by default (decision 19's order), ADR 0009 is Proposed and D2 is open. Its result goes here, and decides the default
  per role and D2.
- The merged analyst is only the comparison's arm: there is no `analyst` role to delegate, and it is not a role file.
- A one-shot specialist cannot read on demand, run `compute`, or read a paper (the skeptical analyst's `source_read`): what
  its input lacks, it can only say is missing. The comparison measures whether that costs valid results.
- The resources are cut at 16 KiB like any record, without an index.

## Acceptance test

1. Start the app with the Centinels provider and open a campaign with an installed problem and the agent team started.
2. On Hypotheses, add a proposal whose algorithm is not installed (for example "quantum annealing of a binary mask" on a
   continuous task).
3. In the chat, ask: "Have the discussion moderator review that proposal with a skeptical domain analyst, a methodology
   specialist and a proposal reviewer, and tell me what they found."
4. Expected: the skeptical analyst and the methodology specialist each finish without tool steps (one call each). Review
   rounds on Hypotheses lists each with a one-line result (such as "3 failure modes, 2 of high severity"), the moderator's
   reply uses them, and Usage lists their cost under their roles. The skeptical analyst names a high-severity failure mode (the method is not installed, or the mask does not
   fit the continuous task); the methodology specialist lists installed methods only.

Live eval (Hoseung, Centinels), at this part's commit, three arms of S12, S14 and S16 with 3 repeats each:

- Sessions: `uv run python scripts/run_lead_evals.py --scenarios S12,S14,S16 --repeats 3 --one-shot-roles proposal_reviewer`
- One-shot: the same without `--one-shot-roles`
- One analyst (D2): the same with `--one-shot-roles proposal_reviewer,analyst`

Compare in each run's `summary.md` the passes and the "Review roles" table (valid results, cost, seconds, input size). A
specialist whose one-shot arm passes fewer scenarios or has fewer valid results than its session goes back to a session in
the default (`DEFAULT_ONE_SHOT`); D2 is taken only if the analyst arm does as well as the one-shot arm at a lower cost.
