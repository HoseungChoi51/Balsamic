# K3 — Findings that say what they state, with typed links

State: implemented and tested (PR #37, 2026-10-27); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

- **A performance claim could rest on a rationale.** A finding recorded its sources, scope and limitations, but not
  whether it stated a promise (why something may work), evidence (what was measured) or a decision (what was chosen, and
  why). An agent could record "restarts beat plain annealing" citing only the proposal and its review.
- **Three agents restating one trial looked like three supports.** A finding had one kind of link,
  `counterevidence_for`, and nothing counted the evidence behind a claim: agreement was counted by findings.
- **A finding could not be replaced.** A corrected finding stood beside the one it corrected, with nothing saying which
  one held.
- **The report writer did not see findings at all**, so it could not keep a promise apart from a result.

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| `finding.record` takes `statement: promise \| evidence \| decision`. An agent must give it; the researcher's findings without one are stored as `unspecified`, and so are findings recorded before K3 when read | `contracts/commands.py` `FindingInput`; `campaigns/findings.py` `record()`, `normalized()` |
| An `evidence` finding cites at least one measurement: an experiment, a reproduction comparison, a confirmation report, a validation result, evaluator evidence, a runtime profile, an equivalence check, a fixed-design job or report, an agent's saved analysis, or a measured asset. A proposal, a review, a source or a reply is not one, and the refusal says to record the argument as a promise | `campaigns/findings.py` `MEASURED`, `measured()`, `record()` |
| A `decision` finding cites the record of the choice: an approval (`decision`), a finalist selection, a reuse decision, a waiver, a budget amendment, a grant, a study activation or a nomination. V1's stops and Phase 3's mandates join the list when they land | `campaigns/findings.py` `DECISIONS` |
| `links: [{id, relation}]` with `supports`, `contradicts`, `supersedes` or `qualifies`, to earlier findings the campaign can see (the same rule as `counterevidence_for`). `counterevidence_for` stays as input and is also stored as `contradicts` links; a finding from before K3 reads its `counterevidence_for` as `contradicts` links | `contracts/commands.py` `FindingLink`; `campaigns/findings.py` `record()`, `links()` |
| Files lists findings through `view()`: each finding with its distinct measurements and those of the findings that support it, two levels deep; each supporter marked `restates` when it cites no source the finding and its earlier supporters did not already cite; a superseded finding moved out of the default list into `superseded_findings`, still readable by id | `campaigns/findings.py` `view()`; `src/balsamic/api/files.py` |
| The Files page shows the statement ("evidence", "promise · not measured", "decision"), "N distinct measurements behind it and its supporting findings", who supports it and who restates, its links, and a closed "Superseded findings (n)" section that opens when a link points at one of them | `frontend/src/files/FilesPage.tsx` |
| The report writer's frozen evidence includes the campaign's findings, with `statement` and `links` in each record and in the inventory summary; a finding citing unreleased evidence is withheld like a decision. One line of the writer's shared instructions says a promise is never reported as a result | `reports/evidence.py` `freeze()`, `summary()`; `reports/editorial.py` |
| The removal summary keeps each finding's statement and links | `campaigns/removal.py` |
| The lead's recording-findings procedure describes `statement` and `links` | `src/balsamic/procedures/recording-findings/SKILL.md` |
| New invariant rows INV-K3-1 (what a finding states and what it must cite) and INV-K3-2 (support counted by distinct measurement; superseded findings out of the default view), next to INV-21 | `docs/invariants.md` |

## Checked

- `tests/test_finding_statements.py` (7 tests): an agent's `evidence` finding citing only a proposal and a review is
  refused with the text, and accepted with a trial; an agent's finding without a statement is refused, the
  researcher's reads as `unspecified`; a `decision` finding needs a decision record; a `promise` is accepted with a
  proposal and a review; `counterevidence_for` is stored as a `contradicts` link, a link to a non-finding is refused, and
  a finding stored before K3 loads as `unspecified` with its `contradicts` link; a superseded finding leaves the default
  view and is still read by id; three findings citing the same trial count as one distinct measurement, the two echoes
  marked as restating, and a second-level supporter citing another trial adds it; the report writer's snapshot carries
  a promise's statement and its `qualifies` link.
- `tests/test_invariant_pins.py`: INV-21's test now gives its agent's finding a statement.
- Playwright `frontend/tests/findings.spec.ts`: the Files page shows the statements, the distinct measurement count, the
  supporters with "(restates)", the links, and the superseded finding apart until a link opens it.
- The full Python suite in parallel, the launcher's tests as `claude`, `npx tsc -b` and the whole Playwright suite: counts
  in the PR.

## Not done

- A stop (V1) and a mandate (Phase 3) are not decision records yet: those parts add their kinds to `DECISIONS`.
- `comparison.report` stores no record, so a comparison cannot be cited as such; the experiments it compares can.
- An agent's saved artifact of kind `analysis` counts as a measurement whatever it holds, since the agent picks the kind.
  K2's numeric checks are the next guard.
- Any finding may supersede an earlier one, including the researcher's: nothing stops an agent's finding from moving a
  researcher's finding out of the default view (it stays readable, and the link shows who superseded it).
- The Notebook tab still shows a finding's kind, not its statement.
- The lead's core instructions are unchanged; only its recording-findings procedure describes the new fields. Whether
  the lead picks the right statement is a live behaviour no eval scenario checks yet.

## Acceptance test

Use a campaign with the lead agent switched on, with at least one finished experiment.

1. In the chat, ask: "Record a finding about what the last experiment showed." Open **Files → Assets**. The new finding
   shows **evidence** and "1 distinct measurement behind it".
2. Ask: "Record as a finding that restarting the search might help, citing only the proposal and its review, and say it
   was measured." The lead cannot record it as evidence: it either records it as a **promise · not measured** (shown so
   on Files, with no measurement count) or tells you it needs an experiment first.
3. Ask: "Record two more findings that support the first one, each citing the same experiment." On Files, both new
   findings show **restates**, and the first finding still says "1 distinct measurement behind it and its supporting
   findings", listing both as "(restates)".
4. Ask: "Record a corrected version of the first finding that supersedes it." The first finding leaves the list; a
   closed **Superseded findings (1)** section appears below. Open it: the old finding is there, with "Superseded by" and
   a link to the new one.
5. Ask the lead to write a report. In the draft, the idea from step 2 is described as untested, not as a result.
