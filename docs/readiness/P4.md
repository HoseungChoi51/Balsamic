# P4 — A second reading: the researcher's words against the charter

State: implemented and tested (PR on branch `claude/project-thread-i4xvqq`, 2026-10-28); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

Layer: none. Nothing failed: after P2 and P3 nothing on the quick path read the researcher's own words again, so a charter the
lead retold wrongly (a changed due time, a dropped limit, a condition they never stated) reached the confirmation with only the
researcher's own read of it to catch the change. On the full path the formulator's reading of their verbatim words is that guard.
Invariant: INV-P4-1, INV-P4-2, INV-P4-3, INV-P4-4 (added).

## Checked

- `tests/balsamic/test_quick_reading.py`: the reading's input holds the researcher's words and the charter and nothing else (not
  the note's confounders, the findings or the lead's citations); its record holds instructions, input, output and usage, with
  the call in `llm_usage` under the lead's run; a revision is read once, a later write again, on the formulator's tier once it
  has a model. A revision with a blocking finding of the lead's is not read; one with only the researcher's (a question) is.
  A difference refuses the confirmation, names its charter path in the gate and the state block; only the researcher settles
  it, and only on the reading's revision; settling records who and how; the confirmation can settle as right in the same
  action, and records nothing when it is refused. Asking the lead starts its turn naming the difference with the
  researcher's words and note, the confirmation waits for its next write, which is read again and confirmed. A difference
  settled as right stays settled for a later reading while the charter states the same there, and not once it changes. The
  confirmation waits while the reading runs, and asks for one when the lead's last write was not read. A reply off its
  schema, a failed call, no model and an over-budget call each end the quick path with the reason.
- `tests/test_lead_evals.py`: S20's charter is read once and nothing is found; a variant whose lead changes the fourth due
  time is caught by the scripted reading, sent back to the lead, fixed, read again and confirmed (message, settle, confirm).
- `tests/balsamic/test_quick_charter.py`, `tests/balsamic/test_quick_fallback.py`: the quick path's tests run with a reading
  that finds nothing; the criteria now include `reading`.
- Full runs at the PR's head: see the PR.

## Acceptance test

On a campaign started from the objective only, with the agent team started and a model on the formulator's tier (Models):

1. In the chat, describe the baking order of S20: "One oven takes 8 bakes, one after another. Their durations in minutes, bake
   0 to 7: 30, 45, 20, 60, 35, 25, 50, 40. Their due times, in minutes from the oven's start: 210, 130, 200, 110, 190, 100, 40,
   120. A bake that ends after its due time is late by the difference. Find the order that minimizes the total lateness. Set
   the problem up so we can start."
2. The lead writes the charter and replies; it says the second reading found no difference, or names each it left.
3. Confirm on the Charter tab: it goes through when the reading found nothing; with a difference, it is refused and names it
   (settling it on the page arrives with P5).
4. Live eval: `uv run python scripts/run_lead_evals.py --scenarios S20,S21`: S20 passes with `reading_calls` 1 in its set-up
   metrics, and S21 still falls back on "deterministic" with no reading.
