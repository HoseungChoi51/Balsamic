# T1 — Reviews as one-shot calls with assembled context

State: implemented and tested (PR to be opened, 2026-10-27); the live S12 run and the before/after measurements are Hoseung's.

## What was wrong

Layer: evidence. A proposal reviewer was a Pi session with read tools, so it could read the other verdicts on the revision it
judged before giving its own, and what a review saw was spread over its session's turns.
Invariant: INV-12 (now `code`); INV-T1-1, INV-T1-2, INV-T1-3 (added).

- `campaign_inspect` showed each proposal's concept-review state ("An independent reviewer requested changes or rejected this
  revision"), the hypothesis it read carried `reviews`, and `evidence_search` listed the other reviewers' artifacts. Part R's
  "Optional independence" left this open.
- What a review was given could only be rebuilt from its tool receipts.

## Checked

- `tests/test_one_shot_reviews.py` (5 tests): the input of a third review of a revision holds neither earlier verdict, nor
  an agent's note naming the revision, nor the parent's verdicts, and holds the parent's required changes; the
  instructions name no tool. Through a scheduling pass, the verdict is recorded as before (the artifact pins the hash,
  `readiness()` gives `revision_required` for a reject, the Review rounds view lists the reviewer with its verdict), the
  asker gets its notice, a message to the reviewer is refused, the `agent_call` record holds exactly the prompt the model was
  sent, and a second pass makes no second call. A call the budget cannot cover is refused before it is made, with no
  verdict. An invalid verdict records nothing, and a run a restart left in flight ends failed without a call. A reviewer is
  delegated exactly one proposal.
- `tests/balsamic/test_one_shot_review_reads.py`: a locked confirmation trial the asker cites reads as its status in the
  input, and with its result after release.
- Tests that delegated a reviewer with no proposal now name one (`tests/test_page_agents.py`, `tests/test_roles.py`,
  `tests/test_agent_discussions.py`); the side-conversation tests of `tests/balsamic/test_threads.py` use a literature
  specialist, since they are about threads, not reviews.
- `scripts/run_lead_evals.py --fake --scenarios S12`: pass, with the verdict from a one-shot call.
- The full Python suite in parallel and the launcher's tests as `claude`: counts in the PR.

## Not done

- **The before/after measurements.** "Done when" asks for the cost, the latency and the share of valid outputs of a review
  before and after, from live S12 runs at the baseline (`61538de`) and at this part's commit. They need a model, so they
  are in the acceptance test below, for Hoseung.
- Only the proposal reviewer is a one-shot role. The review specialists follow in T3, which also decides the harness
  plan's D2.
- A one-shot call in flight cannot be cancelled: stopping the reviewer stops a call that has not started; one that has
  finishes and is recorded.
- A reviewer is now delegated one proposal at a time: "review the five proposals" is five delegations. The moderator's
  and the lead's instructions are unchanged; `delegate`'s description says it.
- What a cited record says is cut at 8 KiB without the index `evidence_read` would give; a reviewer that needs more of a
  record cannot read on.

## Acceptance test

1. Start the app with the Centinels provider and open a campaign with an installed problem and the agent team started.
2. On Hypotheses, add a proposal whose algorithm is not installed (for example "quantum annealing of a binary mask" on a
   continuous task).
3. In the chat, ask: "Have the discussion moderator review that proposal with a skeptical domain analyst and a proposal
   reviewer, and tell me the verdict."
4. Expected: on Hypotheses, Review rounds shows the round with the proposal reviewer and its verdict (revise or reject)
   and its rationale; the proposal shows the verdict; the moderator's reply and the lead's answer report it. The reviewer
   shows no tool steps while it works (one call), and Usage lists its cost under the proposal reviewer.
5. Ask the moderator on its page to "tell the reviewer to also look at the seeds": it should say the reviewer answers
   once, and delegate a new review instead.

Live eval (Hoseung, Centinels): `uv run python scripts/run_lead_evals.py --scenarios S12 --repeats 3` at this part's
commit, and at the baseline tag `baseline` (`61538de`) with `--source` pointing at a checkout of it. Compare pass rates,
and from each run's results the proposal reviewer's cost (Usage), its time from delegation to verdict, and whether its
verdict was valid (recorded, not refused). S12 passes as at the baseline or better.
