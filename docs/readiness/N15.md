# N15 — Algorithm families in proposals and prompts

State: implemented and tested (PR pending, 2026-10-27); not checked live. J1's scenario S17 measures the effect.

## What was wrong

Agents were asked which installed *search* method fits, and nothing asked for the problem's class, its complexity or the
algorithms known for it. A proposal recorded without a method was recorded with hillclimb, as if hillclimb had been
chosen, and could run as such. `diversify` asked only for "a different mechanism", so three metaheuristics passed as a
diverse round, and there was nowhere to say that a proposal is an exact method, a solver or a greedy rule.

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| Proposals take `family`: `exact`, `solver`, `constructive`, `local_search`, `learning` or `hybrid` (or unstated). `hypothesis.edit` can correct it; a library entry keeps it and applying the entry carries it | `contracts/requests.py` (`Family`, `HypothesisInput`), `src/balsamic/commands.py` (`HypothesisEditInput`), `src/balsamic/library/service.py` |
| A proposal without a method records `algorithm = "unchosen"` (not chosen) instead of hillclimb. Its readiness is `unchosen`, not runnable, with "choose one from campaign_inspect.methods"; `hypothesis.allocate` refuses it until a method is chosen. Old records with the hillclimb default keep their "default method" readiness | `campaigns/hypotheses.py` `create()`, `implementations/bridge.py` `readiness()`, `src/balsamic/evolution/interventions.py` `allocate()` |
| The evolution ledger rows and lineage nodes carry `family` | `src/balsamic/evolution/ledger.py`, `lineage.py` |
| Hypotheses list: the family before the method, and "no method chosen" for an unchosen one. The card: a Family row, and "none chosen yet: it cannot run until one is". The lineage graph: the family at a node's top right (with "auto"), and in its tooltip | `frontend/src/hypotheses/HypothesesPage.tsx`, `HypothesisCard.tsx`, `frontend/src/evolution/LineageTree.tsx`, `frontend/src/api/evolution.ts` (`FAMILY`), `api/types.ts` |
| `diversify` asks for strategies of different families where the problem allows, including an exact or problem-specific method when the instance sizes make one plausible; each one's family set, and why a family is left out | `src/balsamic/evolution/generate.py` `PROMPTS` |
| The formulation procedure gains a Known results step: the class in its field's notation, its complexity, the algorithms known for it, with sources, looked up by the literature specialist | `src/balsamic/procedures/problem-formulation/SKILL.md` |
| The proposal-fields procedure documents `family` and `unchosen`, and the method is chosen for the representation and the instance sizes | `src/balsamic/procedures/proposal-fields/SKILL.md` |
| The methodology specialist judges installed methods (not "search methods"): each one's family and how its cost grows with the instance sizes. The literature specialist also looks up a problem's class, complexity and known algorithms | `agents/roles/methodology_specialist.md`, `agents/roles/literature_specialist.md` |
| Invariant rows INV-N15-1 (unchosen, not a default) and INV-N15-2 (the family) | `docs/invariants.md` |

The added role text is paid for under the word ratchet: the lead's instructions stay at 970 words, the methodology
specialist's at 406, and the literature specialist's go from 428 to 425.

## Checked

- `tests/balsamic/test_algorithm_families.py` (7 tests): `hypothesis.create` stores `family` and the ledger, the lineage
  layout, the Hypotheses page and the card return it; an unknown family is refused and `hypothesis.edit` corrects one; a
  proposal without `algorithm` is `unchosen`, not chosen, not executable, its readiness is `unchosen`, `hypothesis.allocate`
  refuses it, and after `hypothesis.edit` chooses `random` it is ready and allocates a run; the `diversify` prompt names the
  six families; the two procedures and two roles say what they should.
- `tests/balsamic/test_housekeeping.py`: its proposal without a lane now expects `unchosen`.
- `tests/test_harness_contract.py::test_instructions_do_not_grow_past_the_ratchet` passes.
- `frontend/tests/algorithm-family.spec.ts`: the list shows "exact ·" and "no method chosen" (never hillclimb), the card
  shows the Family row, the lineage node shows "local search".
- The commands and counts of the full run are in the PR.

## Not done

- The lead is not forced to set a family: it is unstated when left out. The diversify prompt and the procedure ask for it.
- Whether the agents actually propose an exact method on a problem that has one is a live question: J1's scenario S17
  measures it before and after this part, and again after N11 and N13 (live eval below).
- `campaign_inspect.methods` does not yet say which family each installed method belongs to; the specialist judges it.

## Acceptance test

1. Start the app and open a new campaign on "0/1 knapsack" (or baking order: 8 bakes, total tardiness), a problem with a
   known exact algorithm at its size.
2. Before that, while writing the problem note, ask the lead what is known about this problem. It should name the problem's
   class (a 0/1 knapsack; for baking order, single-machine total tardiness, written like 1||ΣTj), its complexity, and the
   algorithms known for it, with sources, and put them in the note's open questions or the charter's references.
3. On the Hypotheses page, ask for three strategies (`/generate 3`). The three cards should each name a family before the
   method (for example "exact ·", "constructive ·", "local search ·"), not three metaheuristics; if a family is left out,
   the lead should say why. One of them should be an exact method (a dynamic program over the capacity or over subsets,
   or branch and bound).
4. Open one card. It should show a Family row. Open it in the graph: the node should show its family at its top right.
5. Ask the lead to record a proposal without choosing a method. Its card should say "no method chosen" (never "hillclimb"),
   its Algorithm row should say it cannot run until one is chosen, and asking to run it should be refused with "choose one".
   Ask the lead to choose a method for it: the warning should go and the proposal should be able to run.
