# B1 — No concept review for proposals the researcher asked for or approved

State: implemented and tested (PR #PR, 2026-10-27); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

- **The review gate blocked runs the researcher asked for.** Every proposal the lead recorded (`hypothesis.create` by the
  `manager` actor), and every library strategy an agent applied, needed an independent concept review of its exact
  revision before a run or a draft could start, even when the researcher had asked for exactly that method in the chat.
- **A go-ahead did not count either.** The researcher could approve a compute plan naming a proposal, and the run still
  waited for a reviewer's "test" verdict.

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| A proposal the lead records in a turn whose request root is a researcher's message records `requested_by = {thread_id, message_id}` (decision 7). Autopilot turns, notice turns whose request no researcher message opened, and other agents' turns record nothing | `campaigns/proposals.py` `researcher_request()`; `campaigns/commands.py` `hypothesis.create`; `src/balsamic/library/service.py` `apply()` |
| The go-ahead of a compute plan sets `approved_by = {decision_id, consent_id}` on each proposal the plan names (its `hypothesis_ids` and its runs' proposals, from B2), unless an earlier go-ahead did | `campaigns/proposals.py` `approve()`, called from `campaigns/approvals.py` `resolve()` |
| `readiness()` returns `eligible: true`, state `not_required`, the reason ("Asked for by the researcher." or "In a plan the researcher approved.") and the record it rests on, when either is set. A review that exists is still listed; an objection is listed under `advisory` and does not block | `campaigns/proposals.py` `readiness()` |
| The proposal card's Review row says "asked for" with a link to the researcher's message, or "approved" with a link to the go-ahead; the proposals list shows the same word | `frontend/src/hypotheses/HypothesisCard.tsx`, `HypothesesPage.tsx` |
| INV-11 now states the two exceptions, pinned by the new tests | `docs/invariants.md` |

## Checked

- `tests/test_asked_for_proposals.py`: a proposal the lead records answering a researcher's message, or in a notice turn
  continuing that request, runs with no review, and a reviewer's later objection shows as advisory; one recorded in an
  autopilot turn, in a notice turn continuing an autopilot request, in a notice turn with no request, by an assistant, or
  with no turn at all still waits for review; a proposal named in an approved plan runs and an objection to it is
  advisory, while a proposal the plan did not name still waits.
- The existing concept-review tests still pass: `tests/test_invariant_pins.py`, `tests/test_pi_agents.py`,
  `tests/test_agent_discussions.py`.

## Not done

- The lead's instructions still describe the review gate for every proposal it records: B7 rewrites them.
- A proposal the lead records is "asked for" whenever its turn answers a researcher's message, whatever the message
  said; it does not check that the message asked for that method (decision 7 settles this reading).
- A researcher's own `trial.create` of an agent's unreviewed proposal is still refused, as before: only the request and
  the go-ahead stand in for the review.

## Acceptance test

Use a campaign with the lead agent switched on and autopilot off.

1. In the chat, write: "Record a proposal for simulated annealing and run it for 2 minutes." The lead records the
   proposal and starts its run without asking for a review. On the Hypotheses page the proposal shows "asked for"; open
   it, and its Review row says "asked for" with a link to your message, and "no concept review needed".
2. Switch autopilot on for a round and let the lead propose methods of its own. Those proposals show "pending" on the
   Hypotheses page, and none of them runs until a reviewer has judged it, as before.
3. Ask the lead to run one of those autopilot proposals for 10 minutes, naming it in the compute request. Approve the
   card in the inbox. The run starts without a review, and the proposal's card shows "approved" with a link to your
   go-ahead.
4. If a reviewer rejects a proposal from step 1 or 3, its card still shows "asked for" or "approved", and its runs are
   not refused.
