# V1 — Typed stop outcomes

State: implemented and tested (PR #32, 2026-10-27); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

Layer: evidence. An agent recorded a stop, a conclusion, without the evidence its gate asks for.
Invariant: INV-14 (updated).

- **A stop was free text.** `hypothesis.status` archived took any `stop_reason` of up to 2,000 characters, or none
  ("stopped without a stated reason"). An under-tuned method and a bad mechanism read the same, on every page.
- **An agent could stop a strategy on nothing.** The lead and autopilot stopped strategies without naming what the stop
  concluded or which records it rested on. The autopilot round prompt asked them to "stop strategies that are dominated".

## Checked

- `tests/test_stop_outcomes.py` (8 tests): the six outcomes and nothing else; an outcome on a status other than archived
  is refused; a stop by the lead or autopilot without an outcome, or without evidence, is refused with the text and
  recorded as a rejection; a stop citing a locked trial, another campaign's trial or an unknown id is refused naming it;
  an agent's stop with an outcome, a trial and a review is recorded with its evidence de-duplicated; the researcher's
  stop without one records `researcher_decision`, and taking the strategy up again clears it; the researcher may give an
  outcome but not cite an invisible record; an old stopped record reads `unspecified` in the ledger.
- `tests/balsamic/test_evolution.py::test_stop_records_a_reason_and_holds_the_race_configuration`: the race configuration
  is still held on a stop. `tests/balsamic/test_autopilot.py::test_away_digest_counts_rounds_stops_promotions_and_waiting_decisions`:
  an autopilot stop with its outcome and evidence reaches the digest with the outcome in words.
- Playwright: `frontend/tests/evolution.spec.ts` (the frontier shows the outcome under the stage; the lineage tooltip reads
  "stopped: adequately assessed, deprioritized") and `frontend/tests/monitor.spec.ts` (the away digest).

## Not done

- The J1 scenario S14 (under-tuned rejection) is not re-run: J1 has not merged, and evals are Hoseung's (below).
- The researcher's Stop dialog on the Evolution page still asks only for a reason; the researcher's stop records
  `researcher_decision`. Choosing an outcome there is left to V5.
- Whether a performance stop followed an adequate assessment (decision 14) is V2's.
- The lead's own instructions say nothing about stops (the size ratchet): an agent learns the two fields from
  `command_schema` for `hypothesis.status`, and a refused stop says what is missing.

## Acceptance test

Use a campaign with the lead agent switched on, a few strategies that have run, and autopilot available.

1. On the Evolution page, Frontier tab, select a strategy, choose Stop…, type a reason and stop it. Its row shows
   "stopped" with "researcher's decision" under it. Hover the stage: "stopped: researcher's decision: <your reason>".
2. Open that strategy's card: it says "Stopped: researcher's decision: <your reason>".
3. In the chat, ask the lead: "Stop the weakest strategy." The lead stops it naming an outcome, for example
   "configuration unpromising" or "under-evaluated", and the row shows that outcome under "stopped". The card shows the
   outcome, the reason and the runs or reviews the stop rests on.
4. Switch autopilot on for a round. Any strategy it stops shows an outcome in words in the frontier table, in the lineage
   tooltip, and, if you were away for more than 30 minutes, in the away digest on the Monitor page ("Stopped: …
   (<outcome>: <reason>; autopilot)").
5. A strategy stopped before this release shows "no outcome recorded" under "stopped".

Live eval (Hoseung's, once J1 has merged and `baseline` is tagged): `uv run python scripts/run_lead_evals.py --scenarios S14`
at this part's merge commit, compared with the same scenario at `baseline`. At `baseline` the lead may stop H02 (one
default configuration) as dominated; after V1 its stop must name an outcome, and the expected one is `under_evaluated`.
