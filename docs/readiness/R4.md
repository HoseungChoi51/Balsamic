# R4 — Autopilot is a standing mandate

State: implemented and tested (2026-10-27); the switch and the cap are checked live by the acceptance test below.

## What was wrong

Layer: mandate. Autopilot was a special case beside the mandates of Phase 3: its own guard (`cap_guard()` in
`src/balsamic/autopilot/rounds.py`), its own settings, and a round prompt that restated the cap. R3's guard checked only the
lead's and the page agents' commands (actor `manager`), so autopilot's work answered to a second rule, and its report date
refused nothing.
Invariant: INV-08 (updated: now held by the mandate guard), INV-R4-1 and INV-R4-2 (new, next to INV-08).

## What changed

| Change | Where |

The switch is recorded as a decision the researcher resolved (`decision_autopilot_…`, "Autopilot on: a compute cap of …, the report by …"), which the mandate names as its go-ahead; it shows under the Inbox's "Recently resolved" and waits for nothing | `src/balsamic/autopilot/settings.py` `grant_mandate()`, `register()` |

Each round's run carries that root with its own run id (`origin.root`), so `governing()` finds the mandate for every round, the notices that continue a round and the page agents it asks, while side conversations and the partnership table keep one key per round | `src/balsamic/autopilot/rounds.py` `start_round()` |
| A change of the cap or the date while on closes the mandate and grants one on the new terms for the same request, so a round at work goes on under them (the cap guard read the settings live). Switching off closes it; switching on again opens a new request | `src/balsamic/autopilot/settings.py` `register()`; `campaigns/mandates.py` `close()` |

## Not done

- Stricter than the cap guard, as decision 11 says: once the cap is spent or the report date has passed, autopilot may only answer, ask
  and record; stops, promotions and new strategies are refused where the cap guard let commands that reserve nothing through. The report
  round writes its report with `report_write`, a tool, not a command, so it is unaffected. R3's `settle()` tells the researcher once in
  the inbox when that end refused autopilot's work.
- The charge estimate counts some work the cap guard reserved nothing for (a run without `wall_seconds` as 60 s, runtime profiles,
  validations, commissions with `compute_seconds`). Work of unknown size that states no seconds is not checked against the cap, as before.
- Autopilot cannot ask for more with a card (the cap, the date, a coding workspace): it says so in the round's summary, and the researcher
  changes the cap with `autopilot.set`.
- R7's Close button, once it exists, would close autopilot's mandate while autopilot stays on: its rounds would start and have their work
  refused. Switching autopilot off is the way to stop it.
- A round that was in its turn when this change was installed has no mandate root; its commands are checked as a request with none
  (Explore) until the next round.
- The mandate has no model-spend envelope of its own; the campaign's LLM cap applies, as before.
- `docs/incident-triage.md` still names `cap_guard()` as autopilot's budget guard; it changes when Phase 3 completes.

## Acceptance test

Start Balsamic with a campaign whose problem is defined (the gate is open) and the lead agent switched on.

1. On the Monitor, switch autopilot on with a cap of 5 minutes and a report date three days ahead. The Inbox shows nothing waiting; under
   "Recently resolved" it lists "Autopilot on: a compute cap of 5 min, the report by … UTC".
2. Before the first round starts, start a 3-minute run yourself on Experiments. Let autopilot run its rounds: its allocations add up to
   at most the 2 minutes left, and the Monitor's autopilot row never shows more spent than the cap. Its commands carry the autopilot badge,
   and the away digest after 30 minutes away reads as before.
3. While a round runs, raise the cap to 10 minutes. The round goes on and may allocate up to the new cap.
4. Once the cap is spent, the next round is the last: it writes the report and starts, stops and promotes nothing more. If it tried, the
   Inbox shows one notice that autopilot's mandate has ended.
5. Switch autopilot off. No further round starts and the campaign's delegation is restored; anything still asked for in the autopilot
   thread is refused.
