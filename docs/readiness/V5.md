# V5 — The gates on the pages; waivers in one click

State: implemented and tested (PR #90, 2026-10-28); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

Layer: UI. The gates V1 to V3 put on agents' stops and promotions held in the commands, but nothing on the pages showed them.
Invariant: INV-V5-1 (new).

- **A refused transition was visible only in the command's error.** When the lead could not stop a proposal as inferior
  (V2) or make it a finalist (V3), the researcher learned why from the lead's reply, if at all. The Evolution page showed
  neither how a proposal had been tried nor whether its best score had passed the fidelity check.
- **A waiver took several commands.** To accept a stop on fewer runs, the researcher had to wait for the agent's card; to
  publish a score whose check never ran, they had to create the requirement (`validation.require`) and then waive it.
- **The researcher's Stop dialog had no outcome.** Every stop from the page recorded "researcher's decision" (V1 left this
  to V5).

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| Each ledger row carries `fidelity` (the V3 state of its best score, with the check, result and waiver it rests on, and words) and `gates`: the assessment gate (an agent's stop as inferior, V2) unless the proposal is stopped, and the fidelity gate (an agent's finalist, and every library entry, V3). A gate is `open`, `waived` (by the researcher) or `closed`; a closed one says why, and what unlocks it: the missing runs, the check to run (`validation.run` payload), or a waiver. Each gate lists its evidence ids: the proposal's runs, the best run, the validation result, the waiver | `src/balsamic/evolution/gates.py`, `src/balsamic/evolution/ledger.py` `rows()` |
| `validation.waive` takes, instead of `requirement_id`, `tuning_hypothesis_id` or `fidelity_hypothesis_id`: the researcher's waiver of that proposal's gate in one step. For the assessment it creates the proposal's `tuning` requirement (as V2's card does) and waives it; for fidelity it waives each check the best score has not passed, creating the requirement on the best run's best design first when none exists (as `validation.require` would). Refused for agents, for an open gate ("no waiver is needed"), and for a proposal with no measured run. The waivers are the usual records | `contracts/commands.py` `WaiverInput`; `campaigns/commands.py` `_waive_gate()`, `_waiver()` |
| The Evolution page's frontier shows the gates under each row's stage ("tried 1 of 3 configurations, 1 seed each", "fidelity not checked"), coloured by state. "Why, and what unlocks it" opens the row's gates: the stop outcome and its evidence, each gate's state with its evidence linked (a run opens its page, any other record its trail of origin), why a closed one refuses and what unlocks it, a "Run the check" button, and a Waive field that needs the researcher's reason | `frontend/src/evolution/Gates.tsx`, `EvolutionPage.tsx` |
| The Stop dialog asks for an outcome (default: the researcher's decision). A stop as inferior on a proposal tried less than the rules ask says so before it goes through: the researcher's own stop is not gated and records how it was tried. "Promote to finalist" on a proposal whose fidelity gate is closed says the warning will be recorded and that the library will need the check or a waiver | `frontend/src/evolution/EvolutionPage.tsx` `InterventionBar`, `Interventions.ts` |
| An agent's waiver card in the Inbox needs the researcher's reason before "Waive"/"Accept the stop"; the reason is recorded with the waiver | `frontend/src/inbox/InboxPage.tsx` `DecisionCard` |
| The proposal card lists its gates and shows the warning a researcher's promotion past a closed gate recorded | `frontend/src/hypotheses/HypothesisCard.tsx` |
| INV-V5-1 (new): only the researcher waives a gate in one step, only while it is closed | `docs/invariants/V5.md` |

## Checked

- `tests/test_evidence_gates.py` (6 tests):
  - a proposal run once shows its assessment gate closed with "1 of 3 configurations; 1 of 2 seeds", "5 runs" to unlock and
    its run as evidence; an agent's one-step waiver is refused and its stop as inferior stays refused; the researcher's
    waiver turns the gate to waived, the agent's stop then goes through with the waiver recorded, and a stopped proposal
    shows no assessment gate;
  - an adequately assessed proposal's gate is open and a waiver is refused as not needed; a problem with no fidelity check
    shows the fidelity gate open, "not required for this problem";
  - a proposal never run cannot be waived through: its assessment gate offers no waiver, its fidelity gate says "Run it
    first", and the fidelity waiver is refused;
  - a 2D deflector score that failed the order check (Part F's numbers) shows the fidelity gate closed ("does not hold at
    higher fidelity", the run and the validation result as evidence); the agent's promotion and one-step waiver are
    refused; the researcher's waiver opens the gate, and the agent's finalist and the library entry go through as waived;
  - an unchecked score names the check to run (`validation.run` with the run, recipe and parameters); the researcher's
    waiver creates the requirement on the best run first, then waives it;
  - a waiver naming two subjects is refused.
- Playwright: `frontend/tests/evolution.spec.ts` (a gated row shows its state, why and what unlocks it; "Run the check"
  sends `validation.run`; a waiver from the row sends `validation.waive` with the reason and the row shows the gate waived;
  the Stop dialog sends the chosen outcome and warns about a stop as inferior on too few runs; Promote warns about the
  unchecked fidelity) and `frontend/tests/inbox-actions.spec.ts` (an agent's waiver card needs a reason).
- The V1, V2 and V3 tests (`tests/test_stop_outcomes.py`, `tests/test_assessment_gate.py`, `tests/test_promotion_fidelity.py`)
  and the invariant and readiness tests pass. Full suite: see the PR.

## Not done

- **An agent's fidelity waiver still takes two commands** (`validation.require`, then `approval.request` with the
  `requirement_id`). Only the researcher's waiver is one step.
- **Revoking a waiver from the row.** A waiver is revoked as before (`validation.revoke_waiver`); the row shows it waived
  with the waiver linked.
- **The lineage tree** shows the stop outcome (V1) but not the gates; they are on the frontier and the proposal card.
- **Other pages.** The Library page shows each entry's fidelity (V3); the Experiments page does not show gates.

## Acceptance test

Use a 2D deflector campaign (the FLRL 2D problem) with the lead agent switched on (with a "Screen" or "Run the campaign"
go-ahead) and two or three proposals, one of which has run only once, at its default settings.

1. Open the Evolution page, Frontier tab. Under each proposal's stage you see "tried" with how it was run (for example
   "1 of 3 configurations, 1 seed each") and "fidelity" with the state of its best score (for example "not checked").
   Closed gates are marked in amber, open ones in green.
2. On the proposal that ran once, click "why, and what unlocks it". It says an agent's stop as inferior is refused because
   it was tried with 1 of 3 configurations and 1 of 2 seeds, and how many runs would unlock it. Its runs are listed as
   evidence; clicking one opens that run.
3. Under "Finalist and library", it says the order-convergence check has not run on the best score. Click "Run the check":
   a check run starts (on the Experiments page). When it finishes, the row shows the fidelity as passed or failed.
4. Back on the first proposal, type a reason in the Waive field under "Stop as inferior" and click Waive. The gate now
   reads "waived by you". Ask the lead in the chat to stop it as unpromising: it goes through without asking you.
5. For a proposal whose check failed or never ran, waive the fidelity gate the same way, with a reason. Ask the lead to
   make it a finalist: it goes through. On the Library page, publish it: the entry's Fidelity row says "waived by the
   researcher".
6. Select a proposal, choose Stop…, pick "configuration unpromising" and type a reason. If it was tried less than the
   rules ask, the dialog says so. Stop it: the row shows "stopped" with "configuration unpromising" under it.
7. Ask the lead to stop another proposal that ran only once as inferior. It will offer to ask you for a waiver: let it.
   In the Inbox, the card "Let … be stopped as inferior on fewer runs?" cannot be accepted until you type a reason.
   Accept it with a reason: the row's gate reads "waived by you".

No live eval: V5 changes what the pages show and how the researcher waives; the gates themselves are V2's and V3's, with
their evals (S13, S14).
