# R2 — `mandate_propose`: the lead's first act on a request

State: implemented and tested (2026-10-27); the mandate card is checked live by the acceptance test below.

## What was wrong

- **The lead decided a request's scope in prose.** What a request allowed beyond compute was a line of the instructions
  ("start one only when a request covers it"), and the only thing the researcher was asked for was compute
  (`approval.request` kind `compute`) once work passed the go-ahead threshold.
- **R1 recorded mandates, but nothing proposed one**, so no request had a mandate the later parts (R3's guard, R7's dial)
  could check or show.

## Checked

- `tests/test_mandate_propose.py` (6 tests): an Explore mandate under the threshold is granted at once, named in the tool's
  receipt and in the state block, and Answer too, while Explore over the threshold or with a coding workspace is refused; a
  Screen mandate becomes one card with the three choices and its envelope, deadline and stop condition, and approving it records
  the mandate with the decision, its consent and the approved proposal, and the state block shows what is left; "Approve as
  Explore only" records Explore with no consent, and Decline records nothing; a request's first work-starting command records
  Explore once, and asking records nothing; a page agent asked on its page proposes its own mandate and its run is charged to
  it, while one the lead asked is refused; an agent's kind `compute` is refused with `mandate_propose` named.
- Playwright `inbox-actions.spec.ts`: the mandate card shows its template and envelope and the three choices, and "Approve as
  Explore only" sends choice 1.
- `tests/test_compute_consent.py`, `tests/test_implementation_ceiling.py`, `tests/test_pi_development_workspaces.py` (their
  refusal texts now name `mandate_propose`), `tests/test_mandates.py`, `tests/test_harness_contract.py` (the prompt ratchet)
  and `tests/test_turn_state.py` pass.

## Not done

- The bus does not refuse work outside a mandate (R3): an exhausted or expired mandate, and a class a template does not
  allow, still start as before. Until R3 the go-ahead is enforced by the consent, as in B2.
- Model spend is not charged to a mandate (R5), and the chat has no mandate dial (R7). Autopilot is not a standing mandate (R4).
- The lead's instructions still say in prose that stages start only when a request covers them; R6 removes what the guard
  enforces.
- Newest wins: a smaller mandate proposed after a larger one in the same request becomes the active one. R3 decides whether a
  request may narrow its own mandate.
- The default Explore is recorded at a request's first work-starting command, after it ran; R3 checks the command against it.

## Acceptance test

Start Balsamic with a campaign that has a problem and a proposal, and the lead agent switched on.

1. In the chat, ask the lead something it can answer from the records ("Which methods are installed?"). It answers, and
   nothing new appears in the Inbox.
2. Ask for a small piece of work below your go-ahead threshold (Monitor, Resources; 5 minutes by default), such as "Run two
   one-minute probes of coordinate search". The runs start without a card in the Inbox: the request works under Explore,
   by default or as a mandate the lead names in one line of its reply.
3. Ask for work beyond the threshold, such as "Build the proposal and screen it against coordinate search with two seeds".
   One card appears in the Inbox: "Approve a Screen mandate for this request?", saying what will run and why, the worker time,
   the coding workspace, the deadline, and three buttons: Approve, Approve as Explore only, Decline. Nothing starts yet.
4. Press "Approve as Explore only". The lead is told and keeps to work below the threshold, with no coding workspace; it does
   not start the long screen.
5. Ask again for the screen, and press Approve on the new card. The lead starts the coding workspace and the runs.
6. Ask for something large once more and press Decline. Nothing starts, and the lead says so.
