# P5 — Screens: the quick note and the confirm card

State: implemented and tested (PR on branch `claude/project-thread-i4xvqq`, 2026-10-28); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

Layer: UI. The pages showed the full path only: after P2 to P4 the lead's charter was confirmed on the Charter tab, a page built
for the formulator's charter, and nothing in the conversation put it before the researcher; the note page did not say whether
the quick path held, and nothing on a page settled what the second reading found (P4). Answering the lead's questions and then
confirming at once confirmed a charter the lead had not yet revised from the answers.
Invariant: INV-P5-1, INV-P5-2 (added).

## What changed

| Change | Where |

## Checked

- `tests/balsamic/test_quick_card.py`: a read revision is put before the researcher as a card in the thread the lead wrote in,
  after their words; another write of the same turn moves that card to its revision where it stands; a write with a blocking
  finding of the lead's has none; a later turn has a card of its own. A write the quick path does not hold for has no card.
  The view's `why` is the refusal's own words (an open question, a difference), none once settled, and the view says when the
  charter is confirmed. A request starts a lead turn and the confirmation waits (`REVISING`) until the lead writes again, though
  its turn still runs; a turn that ends without writing leaves the last revision to confirm.
- `frontend/tests/quick-charter.spec.ts` (8 tests): a quick note is read-only with no Adopt, lists its criteria, and "Use the
  full path" (asked once more) makes it a note to edit and adopt that says why the quick path ended; the card shows the
  baking order in plain words and confirms in one click, then says the gate is open; a card the lead wrote over is disabled
  and points at the newest card, or at the Charter tab when there is none; a question is answered on the card, which then waits
  for the lead's revision; differences are settled on the card (ask the lead with words, the charter is right), and the Charter
  tab lists the same ones and says why its Confirm waits; a settled difference shows how; a card whose quick path ended says why.
- `tests/balsamic/test_quick_charter.py`, `test_quick_fallback.py`, `test_quick_reading.py`, `tests/test_lead_evals.py`: the
  quick path's tests pass with the card and the wait on the lead.
- Full runs at the PR's head: see the PR.

## Not done

- The card does not list the tasks confirming installs; the Charter tab and the gate show them once confirmed.
- A turn of the lead that stays queued (the team paused, the agent service down) keeps the confirmation waiting until it ends;
  the researcher can stop it or take the full path.

## Acceptance test

On a campaign started from the objective only, with the agent team started and a model on the formulator's tier (Models):

1. In the chat, describe the baking order of S20 (as in P4). After the lead's reply, a card in the chat shows the problem in
   plain words: minimize the total lateness, the order of the 8 bakes as what is chosen, the durations and due times as you gave
   them, the new problem that computes the score, and any assumption confirming accepts.
2. If the card lists a difference the second reading found, settle it on the card: "The charter is right", or "Ask the lead to
   fix it" (the lead writes again and a new card follows; the old one says it is out of date and "Show the newest" scrolls to it).
3. Click "Confirm and set the tasks" on the card: it says the charter is confirmed and the gate is open (the Overview's next
   step moves on from the charter).
4. On another campaign, open the Problem note while the lead's charter waits: the sections are read-only, with the charter
   revision they come from, the quick path's checks, and "Use the full path"; using it makes the note editable with Adopt and
   says why the quick path ended.
