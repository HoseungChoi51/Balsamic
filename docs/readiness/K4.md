# K4 — Negative results in the library

State: implemented and tested (PR #89, 2026-10-28); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

Layer: evidence. A method stopped after a fair assessment left no trace another campaign could find, so the next campaign
of the same problem could try it again without knowing it had been tried, how, and why it was stopped.
Invariant: INV-K4-1 and INV-K4-2 (new, in [K4.md](../invariants/K4.md)).

- **The library held only winners.** `library.publish` took a finalist or a strategy whose confirmation was promising.
  A strategy stopped as unpromising after three configurations and two seeds each was invisible outside its campaign.
- **Nothing kept a negative result honest.** With typed stops (V1) and the adequacy gate (V2) a stop can now say what it
  concluded and on how many runs, but nothing carried that conclusion, with its limits, to another campaign.

## Checked

- `tests/test_library_negative.py` (7 tests):
  - a stop as `configuration_unpromising` after three configurations × two seeds publishes as negative, with the stop,
    the assessment (basis adequate, each configuration's seeds), the best score and the signature, immutable; a stop as
    `under_evaluated` is refused with the reason and writes nothing;
  - an unstopped strategy, `unaffordable_within_allocation`, the researcher's stop without an outcome and a stop from
    before outcomes are refused, each with the reason;
  - the researcher's own stop as `configuration_unpromising` on one run is refused for the library, naming what is
    missing, until the researcher waives the minimums; the entry then records the waiver;
  - `implementation_invalid` without evidence ids is refused; with them it publishes (assessment not required);
  - the lead and autopilot cannot publish a negative entry;
  - a second campaign of the problem finds the entry labelled "assessed and stopped: configuration unpromising", the
    stop's reason is searchable, the lead's turn state lists it apart, and applying it writes the outcome, reason,
    assessment, origin campaign and problem into the new proposal's rationale;
  - an old entry without a kind reads as positive and stays on the winners' line.
- `tests/balsamic/test_library.py` (positive entries, search, apply, the lead's first reply) passes unchanged.
- Playwright `frontend/tests/library.spec.ts`: the two kinds show apart, with the negative card's outcome, assessment and
  evidence; a strategy stopped as adequately assessed is offered for publishing as assessed and stopped and sends
  `library.publish` with `kind: negative`. The whole Playwright suite passes.
- Full suite: see the PR.

## Not done

- A negative entry carries no fidelity record: its score is not offered for reuse, so the V3 check does not apply.
- Nothing in the lead's instructions tells it to look for negative entries before proposing (the size ratchet); it sees
  them in its turn state and in `library_search`.
- No live check: Playwright for K4 runs against the mocked workspace.

## Acceptance test

Use two campaigns of the same problem (for example two on the bounded quadratic), the first with a strategy that has run
in three configurations with two seeds each.

1. In the first campaign, stop that strategy as "configuration unpromising" (ask the lead: "Stop <strategy> as
   configuration unpromising, citing its runs"). Its row on the Evolution page shows "configuration unpromising" under
   "stopped".
2. Open the Library page. Under "Publish from this campaign" the strategy appears with "stopped: configuration
   unpromising" and "3 of 3 configurations, 2 seeds each". Click "Publish as assessed and stopped".
3. It appears under "Assessed and stopped (1)", apart from "Strategies that won", with the reason, the assessment, its
   best score as measured and the runs it rests on.
4. Stop another strategy that ran only once as "under-evaluated" and try to publish it the same way: it is not offered,
   and if forced through the chat the app refuses with "its stop outcome is under-evaluated, which says nothing about the
   method".
5. Open the second campaign's Library page: the entry is there, labelled "assessed and stopped: configuration
   unpromising", with "same problem" under why it fits. Click "Apply here…", then Apply: the new strategy's card says
   in its rationale why and where the method was stopped.
6. In the second campaign's chat, say hello to the lead: it can mention the method as tried and stopped elsewhere, not as
   a winner.
