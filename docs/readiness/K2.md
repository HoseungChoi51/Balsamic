# K2 — Numeric claim checks

State: implemented and tested (PR #59, 2026-10-27); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

Layer: evidence. Nothing compared the numbers an agent wrote with the numbers in its evidence: in readiness Part J the
operator called a mutation rate of 0.005 "about 1/n" for 32,768 cells, where 1/n is 0.0000305, and the report writer's
rule that numbers "must be traceable to supplied read receipts" was prose.
Invariant: INV-19 (updated: `prompt`, measured), INV-K2-1 (added).

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| `numbers(text)` extracts decimals, numbers with thousands separators, percentages, scientific notation and signs, and skips numbers in record ids (and other identifiers with digits, such as H01), dates, times, versions, links and code spans, and integers below 10 (counts). `unverified(text, evidence)` returns the numbers no evidence number rounds to at the precision written, directly or as a percentage (93.75% and 0.94 match 0.9375; 0.95 does not). A number written without a sign may match a negative one ("fell by 3.2"); a sign written must match | `analysis/claims.py` (new) |
| An agent's `finding.record`: its content against its sources, the findings it links, and the recording request's tool receipts and text. The unmatched numbers are saved on the finding (and its published asset) as `unverified_numbers`, and the command's outcome names them with a note to cite their record or use compute. The researcher's own findings are not checked | `campaigns/findings.py` `unverified_numbers()` |
| `yield_work(complete)`'s report: against the request's tool receipts (the turn asked and the notice turns that continue it) and the records they returned, the records the reply cites in `evidence_ids`, what its assistants delivered since the request began, and the request's own text. Saved on the call's receipt and the run's disposition, and copied onto the chat message | `agents/tools.py` `PiTools.unverified_numbers()`; `agents/controller.py` (the message) |

## Checked

- `tests/test_numeric_claims.py` (5 tests): extraction of percentages, separators, scientific notation and signs, with ids,
  dates, times, versions, links, code spans and counts skipped; matching at the precision written (93.75%, 0.94 and 94%
  match 0.9375, 0.95 does not; a written sign must match); an agent's finding with a number in none of its sources is
  saved with `unverified_numbers` (and Files lists it), one with all its numbers sourced is saved without it, and the
  researcher's finding is not checked; a compute receipt of the request sources a finding's number, and a reply with a
  number from nowhere has it on the yield_work receipt and on the chat message; a report claim whose number is in no
  supporting receipt is listed on its investigation stage and on the claim.
- `tests/test_lead_evals.py`: `--fake` runs every scenario including S16, whose three checks pass with the ratio from
  compute; the summary has the unverified-numbers column; `reply_numbers_verified` fails on a reply with a badge.
- Playwright `frontend/tests/unverified-numbers.spec.ts`: the badge on a finding and on a reply, and none where every
  number is sourced.
- `tests/test_invariants.py`: the new and updated rows' pins exist (its anchor for INV-19 is now `analysis/claims.py`
  instead of `| K2 |`).
- The full Python suite in parallel, the launcher's tests as `claude`, `npx tsc -b` and the whole Playwright suite: counts
  in the PR.

## Not done

- T2's synthesis reasons are not checked: T2 has not landed. When it does, its synthesis is one more `unverified()` call.
- No refusal: badges come first, as Phase 9 and the design review ask. INV-19 becomes `code` only if a later part refuses.
- False badges will happen: units (a time written in minutes from seconds), rounding to tens ("about 1,200" for 1,187)
  and numbers the model derives correctly without compute. The metric shows the rate; the live comparison against the
  Phase 2 baseline is Hoseung's.
- Only the content of a finding is checked, not its limitations or scope.
- A page agent's reply to the lead is checked like the lead's; a specialist's structured `submit_output` is not.
- S16's run is a saved record, not a real run: no installed problem without MEENT has 32,768 cells. On the `deflector_2d`
  preset (256 × 128) a real run would be possible.
- `docs/status.md` still lists INV-19 as `none` (I3's rule: only the part completing a phase edits it).

## Acceptance test

Use a campaign with the lead agent switched on and at least one finished experiment.

1. In the chat, ask: "What is the best score of the last experiment?" The reply should give the score shown on
   Experiments, with no "unverified numbers" badge next to the reply.
2. Ask: "Record a finding that the last experiment reached its best score, and that a longer run would reach 0.99."
   Open **Files → Assets**: the new finding shows a badge "1 unverified number: 0.99" (the best score itself has no
   badge, since the experiment holds it).
3. Ask: "Without reading anything, tell me roughly what 0.005 times 32,768 is, as a plain number." If the reply states a
   number that was not computed (for example "about 160"), it shows a badge naming it; if the lead used "Computing a
   number" first, it shows none.
4. Ask the lead to write a report. When it is ready, open it and expand **Writing notes & evidence limits**: any claim
   whose number none of its supporting reads holds has an "Unverified numbers" line under it; the others have none.
5. Live eval (Hoseung's): `uv run python scripts/run_lead_evals.py --scenarios S16,S12,S14 --compare <the Phase 2
   baseline>` at this PR's merge commit. S16 should pass; compare the "Unverified numbers" column and the suite line
   with the baseline (which has none, as the metric is new: record it as this part's starting rate).
