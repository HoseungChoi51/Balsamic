# P2 — The lead writes the charter; the note is read from it; one confirmation

State: implemented and tested (PR on branch `claude/project-thread-i4xvqq`, 2026-10-28); not checked live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

Layer: tools and commands. For a simple problem two agents wrote two artifacts about the same problem, the lead the note
and the formulator the charter after Adopt, and the researcher acted three to five times between them (S20 on the scripted
harness: the message, Adopt, the confirmation).
Invariant: INV-P2-1 to INV-P2-5 (added).

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| The lead's `charter_write` (decisions 28, 29): a whole charter; the first call creates the campaign's charter (`writer: lead`, source the campaign and the thread) and puts the note on the quick path (`path: quick`), a later call revises it. It returns the computed values, the texts with values and the findings. It keeps the formulator's rules: a value the researcher set stays, answers and waivers are kept, only the researcher answers, accepts, waives and confirms. It takes the note's confounders and at most two questions (a new question in the charter counts as asked). | A note's `path`: none until written, `full` once the note is revised or adopted, `quick` once the lead writes the charter. On the quick path the note's view is read from the charter's current revision: objective (the text and the objective), knobs (the decision variables and every quantity with its value), observables (objective, metrics, the evaluator), noise (deterministic or not, fidelity), constraints, the lead's confounders, and open questions (the charter's questions, assumptions and assumed values). Refused on a stale revision, an open question (or one left to the lead, which is deciding it), any other blocking finding, and once confirmed and installed.   adopting and skipping are refused; a second call revises; `charter_read` reads it back. `problem_note.confirm` refuses a
  stale revision, then accepts the assumption, confirms and installs the task of the declared problem in one command; the
  gate opens and the stage moves on; confirming again and a later `charter_write` are refused. A blocking finding other

## Not done

- When the quick path holds, and the fall back to the formulator with the lead's draft, are P3: until then the lead decides
  from the procedure's "When", and a note on the quick path stays there.
- The check of the researcher's words against the charter is P4.
- The confirm card in the chat and the page for a stale or busy state are P5. The question findings still say "Left to the
  formulator to decide" in the charter's checks.
- Once confirmed, a change requested in words on the lead's charter goes to a formulator, as for any confirmed charter.

## Acceptance test

On a campaign started from the objective only (no tasks, no note), with the agent team started:

1. In the chat, describe a small problem with every number: "One oven takes 8 bakes, one after another. Their durations in
   minutes, bake 0 to 7: 30, 45, 20, 60, 35, 25, 50, 40. Their due times: 210, 130, 200, 110, 190, 100, 40, 120. A bake that
   ends after its due time is late by the difference. Find the order that minimizes the total lateness. Set the problem up
   so we can start."
2. The lead replies with what the charter states, one line per assumption, and a link to the Charter tab. The Problem note
   page shows the "quick path" pill and the sections filled from the charter, with no Adopt button.
3. On the Charter tab, "Confirm and set the tasks" is enabled; press it. The gate opens: the next-step callout goes, the
   campaign moves to generate, and the Problem note reads confirmed.
4. Live eval: `uv run python scripts/run_lead_evals.py --scenarios S20,S21` and compare "Words to a confirmed charter" with
   the P1 baseline: S20 should show two researcher actions and no formulator turn.
