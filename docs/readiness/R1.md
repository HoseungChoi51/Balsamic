# R1 — The mandate record and its templates

State: implemented and tested (PR #PR, 2026-10-27); nothing to check live (see the acceptance test).

## What was wrong

- **Nothing recorded what a request allows except its compute.** A compute consent (B2) names a plan's proposals, runs,
  worker time and coding workspaces; everything else a request allows (whether it may propose, run, implement, decide) was
  a line of the lead's instructions (`agents/tools.py`: "start one only when a request covers it").
- **No record had a class of work, a model-spend envelope, a deadline or a stop condition**, so the later parts of Phase 3
  (R2's proposal, R3's guard, R4's standing mandate, R5's model spend) had nothing to check against.

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| `Mandate`: id, campaign, the request it serves (`root`: thread, message, run; none for a standing mandate), template, classes, plan, proposals, workspaces, planned runs, compute envelope, model-spend envelope, deadline, stop condition (text, and optionally `all_runs_terminal`), status (`proposed`, `active`, `exhausted`, `expired`, `closed`), who granted it (`automatic` or a decision id), use, and the consent that is its compute part | `contracts/mandates.py` |
| `CLASSES`: each of the 97 command operations belongs to one of ten classes (answer, propose, review, implement, run, validate, decide, record, configure, ask); a batch of interventions is checked by its items (`classes_of`) | `campaigns/mandates.py` |
| `TEMPLATES` (decision 10): Answer = {answer, ask}, no compute; Explore = Answer + {propose, review, run, validate, record} up to the go-ahead threshold in total, no coding workspace; Screen = Explore + {implement, decide}; Run the campaign = every class. Screen and Run the campaign are recorded only under a researcher's decision | `campaigns/mandates.py` `TEMPLATES`, `envelope()`, `grant()` |
| Explore's envelope is read from the campaign's thresholds when the mandate is read, so it follows the researcher's changes | `envelope()`, `compute_consent.thresholds()` |
| `active(workspace, campaign_id, root)` gives the newest active mandate serving a request (its own, or a standing one); None means the default, Explore, which R2 records | `active()`, `serves()`, `same_root()` |
| `charge()` counts compute on the consent that is a mandate's compute part, with the consent's own accounting (`compute_consent.spend()`, factored out of `charge()`), or on the mandate itself; model dollars on the mandate | `mandates.charge()`; `compute_consent.spend()` |
| Status is derived when read: expired past the deadline or when its consent is no longer in force, exhausted when its compute or model envelope is spent. `stop_reached()` checks `all_runs_terminal` | `_status()`, `stop_reached()` |
| Every compute consent no mandate names reads as a Screen mandate with only a compute envelope, its plan, proposals, runs and workspaces; a consent recorded before B2 is a standing mandate. A view over the record, never a rewrite | `from_consent()`, `mandates()` |
| INV-R1-1 to INV-R1-3, next to INV-01 | `docs/invariants.md` |

Nothing outside `campaigns/mandates.py` reads mandates yet: the bus, the lead and the approval flow behave as before.
`campaigns/approvals.py` is unchanged; a compute approval still records a consent, which reads as a Screen mandate.

## Checked

- `tests/test_mandates.py` (7 tests): every operation the full app's bus lists, and every declared operation, has a class
  (a new one without a class fails); the templates are decision 10's; a consent recorded before B2 and one recorded after it
  read as Screen mandates with their plan, seconds, workspaces and runs, and the consents are left unchanged, with no
  mandate recorded; Explore's envelope follows a researcher's change of the go-ahead threshold and is exhausted once spent; a mandate
  serves its own request (and a notice turn continuing it), a standing one every request; Screen needs a decision, Explore
  refuses an envelope of its own, unknown proposals are refused; a Screen mandate's compute is charged on its consent by the
  bus, its model dollars on itself, and its model envelope exhausts it; `all_runs_terminal` holds once its run finishes; a
  mandate past its deadline is expired, and charging one without a consent records its workspaces and runs.
- `tests/test_compute_consent.py` and `tests/test_invariants.py` pass unchanged.

## Not done

- Nothing proposes a mandate (R2), the bus does not refuse work outside one (R3), autopilot is not yet a standing mandate
  (R4), and model spend is not charged to one (R5). Until R3 an expired or exhausted mandate refuses nothing.
- The stop condition is recorded and checked by `stop_reached()`, but nothing closes a mandate when it holds.
- Classes the roadmap does not name are a judgement call, written in `CLASSES`: `agent.*` researcher controls, threads and
  campaign settings are `configure`; `literature.search`, `resources.estimate` and `bundle.inspect` are `answer`;
  `reproduction.compare` and `trial.validate` are `validate`; `decision.resolve`, waivers and `confirmation.release` are
  `decide`. R3 may move one when it checks them.

## Acceptance test

None: R1 records and reads mandates, and nothing in the app shows or checks them until R2 (the lead proposes a mandate) and
R7 (the mandate dial in the chat). It shows in `tests/test_mandates.py`. What a user can check now is that nothing changed:
the compute go-ahead of B2 works as before (B2's acceptance test), including an approval given before this change.
