# A2 — Rank by the objective's direction

State: implemented and tested (PR to be numbered, 2026-10-27); not live, the acceptance test is Hoseung's.

## What was wrong

- Within a run the best was already right (the worker sorts its archive by `utility`), but every reader across runs took
  the largest value: the strategy ledger (`score > best`), the evolution page's "best so far" (`max`), a panel chart's
  best run (`max(..., key=best_objective)`) and the Evolution table's sort (`b.best.score - a.best.score`). On a minimize
  campaign the worst strategy was shown as the best.
- The comparison report's method summaries had a mean and a median but no order, so whoever read it had to know the
  direction to rank them.

## What changed

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| `direction_of(trial, task)`: the direction a run is ranked in, from its result's `objective_definition`, else its problem's, else its task's primary objective; a record that names none predates objective definitions and counts as maximize (efficiency) | `src/balsamic/evolution/ledger.py` |
| `best_of(entries)`: the best under one shared direction with `Objective.better`; entries that disagree on direction have no single best, never a mixed comparison | `src/balsamic/evolution/ledger.py` |
| Each ledger row's best is chosen under its runs' direction, and `best` carries `direction` | `src/balsamic/evolution/ledger.py` `rows()` |
| `best_so_far` follows the same rule and the progress block says its `direction`; `None` when strategies disagree | `src/balsamic/api/evolution.py` |
| A panel chart of a strategy draws its best run under the direction | `src/balsamic/panels/service.py` `_chart_points()` |
| Each comparison group names its `direction`; each method summary gets `rank` (1 is best by mean, ties share a rank, no mean no rank) | `analysis/general.py` `rank()`, `report()` |
| The lead's comparison brief carries each group's direction | `agents/tools.py` `comparison_brief()` |
| The Evolution table sorts by the strategies' direction (no score sort when they disagree) and says "lower is better" beside "Best (measured)" when minimizing | `frontend/src/evolution/EvolutionPage.tsx`, `frontend/src/api/evolution.ts` |
| Register row INV-29 | `docs/invariants.md` |

The "Done when" search (`best.score`, `best_objective`, `max(`, `sorted(` in `src/balsamic/evolution`, `src/balsamic/api`,
`src/balsamic/panels`, `frontend/src/evolution`) finds no other reader that ranks by score: the remaining `max(` and
`sorted(` calls order by time, rung or name, and `LineageTree.tsx` only prints the value.

## Checked

- `tests/balsamic/test_evolution.py`: on a minimize campaign the ledger picks the lowest score and its trial, the task's
  objective decides when the result names none, and `best_so_far` is the lowest; strategies that disagree on direction
  give no `best_so_far`; a panel chart draws the lowest-scoring run; the existing ledger test now expects
  `direction: maximize`.
- `tests/test_objective_direction.py`: ranks put the lowest mean first when minimizing and the highest when maximizing,
  ties share a rank and a method without a value has none; a real minimize report (bounded continuous) names its
  direction and ranks the lower mean first.
- Playwright `frontend/tests/evolution.spec.ts`: a maximize campaign lists the highest score first with no label; a
  minimize campaign lists the lowest first, says "lower is better", and shows the lowest as best so far.
- Full runs at this branch: Python 940 passed, 96 skipped with `-n 4` (the known flaky
  `test_supervisor_death_retains_committed_evidence_and_reconciles_without_execution` failed once and passed alone), the
  launcher's 17 as `claude`, the agent harness's 35, `npx tsc -b`, and 98 Playwright tests.

## Acceptance test

1. Start Balsamic and open (or create) a campaign whose objective is minimized, for example a cost, a time or an error
   ("lower is better" in the charter). Let at least two strategies run so each has a measured score.
2. Open **Evolution → Frontier**. The strategy with the lowest score is listed first, the "Best (measured)" column says
   "lower is better", and "best so far" at the top shows the lowest score.
3. Open a campaign whose objective is maximized (for example grating efficiency). The highest score is listed first, and
   the column has no "lower is better".
4. In the minimize campaign, ask the lead in the conversation to compare the methods that ran. The method it names as best
   is the one with the lowest mean.
