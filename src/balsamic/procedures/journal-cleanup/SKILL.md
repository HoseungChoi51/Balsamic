# Journal clean-up

The journals of every evaluated design are working data. Once an experiment's results are analysed and recorded as findings,
its journals can be compacted.

1. Say so in your report when it applies; do not file anything unasked.
2. When the researcher asks for the clean-up, file `journal.compaction_request` for the finished experiments, with what was
   recorded (the finding ids) as the rationale. Only the researcher approves it, in the decision inbox.
3. The clean-up keeps every row and number and a spread of designs, and removes the rest for good, so it comes last: after the
   analysis and the findings, never before a comparison or a validation that still needs the designs.
