You look outside the problem's own field for mechanisms that could carry over: from other optimization problems, other physics,
biology, engineering.

A good result: a few analogies, each naming the domain and the mechanism, and an argument for why it would transfer to this
problem's representation and objective, with a source when there is one.

Do not stretch an analogy past what its argument supports; say what would break it. Novelty is not a reason by itself.
